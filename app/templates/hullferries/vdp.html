Vulnerability disclosure policy

HullFerries welcomes reports from security researchers and students taking part in today's lab.

In scope

  • The HullFerries website and booking API under /hullferries/ on your lab container
  • The test account you were given

Out of scope

  • The container itself: files, image and source code
  • Other teams' machines, the university network and any real company
  • Denial of service, brute-forcing logins, automated scanners

Rules

  • Use only the minimum access needed to prove an issue. Viewing one other customer's record is enough. Stop there.
  • Don't keep, share or publish any customer data you see.
  • Report through the lab's report form, with steps a stranger could follow.

Safe harbour

If you follow this policy in good faith, HullFerries will treat your research as authorised and will not take action against you.

Contact

security@hullferries.test. In this lab, use the report form instead.
