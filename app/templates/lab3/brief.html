The plan

  1. 7 minutes: find it. Log in, look at your own booking, and work out how the site fetches it.
  2. Prove it with minimum access. When it works, the response contains a lab proof token. One record is enough.
  3. 8 minutes: report it. Use the seven-part report form and include the proof token as evidence. Download it and email it to John.smith@dfds.com.
