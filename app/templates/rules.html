Rules of engagement

This is your permission. Stay inside it.

In scope

  • Everything served by this lab at http://localhost:8080
  • The ten fictional organisations in Lab 1 (path-based or *.localhost)
  • The HullFerries web app and its API in Lab 3
  • Your browser, DevTools and curl

Out of scope

  • The container itself: its files, image layers and source code
  • Other teams' machines, the university network and any real website
  • Scanners and brute-forcing tools, unless a lab tells you to use them
  • Anything that slows the lab down for others (denial of service)

Why the container is out of scope

You could open the container and read the answers. In the real world that's like breaking into the company's office to read their bug tracker: it proves nothing about the bug and puts you outside your permission. Find it the way a researcher would.

Keep notes

Write down what you did and when. You'll need it for the Lab 3 report, and in real life it's what protects you if anyone asks.
